Part D Reading (20 marks)

D1 Read the text and answer the questions. (12 marks)

My Weekend and My School

Hello! I am Sam. I am Primary 2. On Saturday morning, I go shopping with Mum. We buy bread at the bakery. We buy fruit at the fruit shop. Mum buys a storybook for me at the bookstore.

On Sunday, I play football in the park with Dad. Sometimes I watch TV at home.

My school is Happy Primary School. There is a big playground and a library. My classroom is on the second floor. It is next to the music room. I like my teacher, Miss Chan. She is kind.

Yesterday I lost my blue water bottle. I looked under my desk. Then I found it in the lost-and-found box near the canteen. I was happy!

  1. Where does Sam buy bread? (2)
  2. What does Mum buy for Sam at the bookstore? (2)
  3. What does Sam do on Sunday? (2)
  4. Where is Sam’s classroom? (2)
  5. What colour is Sam’s water bottle? (2)
  6. Where did Sam find his water bottle? (2)
