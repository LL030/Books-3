教師用資料 Teacher Pack

Primary 2 English Quarterly Test · Living Oxford English 2A Units 1–4
含：命題範圍說明、聽力錄音稿、標準答案、寫作評分指引

使用說明：本卷依香港小學常用 Living Oxford English 2A 上學期主題 （Shopping Around、Weekend Fun、My School、Lost and Found）原創命題， 並非出版社原版試卷。若貴校默書字表／課文句型略有不同，請替換 Part B 詞彙後再施測。

1. 命題範圍 Scope

單元主題詞彙焦點句型／語法焦點
Unit 1 Shopping Around bakery, bookstore, toy shop, supermarket, fruit shop, buy… I buy … at the … / How much is/are …?
Unit 2 Weekend Fun Saturday, Sunday, park, football, swimming, watch TV, visit… What do you do at the weekend? / on Saturday/Sunday
Unit 3 My School classroom, library, playground, canteen, hall, teacher, uniform There is/are … / next to / Where is …?
Unit 4 Lost and Found bag, pencil, eraser, ruler, umbrella, water bottle, belongings Whose … is this? / my, your, his, her / It’s …’s

2. 聽力錄音稿 Listening Scripts

每段播放 兩次。語速宜慢，咬字清晰。括號內為停頓提示。

A1（選擇題）

1. Mum: I need some bread for breakfast. Let’s go to the bakery.

2. Tom: On Sunday I play football with my friends in the park.

3. Girl: Excuse me. Where is the library?
Boy: It’s next to the playground.

4. Teacher: Whose bag is this? It has “Ken” on it.
Children: It’s Ken’s bag!

A2（填空）

Hello! My name is Amy. I like shopping. I buy books at the bookstore. I buy apples at the fruit shop. At the weekend, I play in the park with my sister. My school is big. There is a library and a playground. Yesterday I lost my bag. It is blue. Is this yours?

A3（剔選）

1. Dad: I want to buy a new shirt at the clothes shop.

2. May: On Saturday I go to the cinema with my family.

3. Ben: I lost my ruler. Oh! Here it is — under the desk!

E2 評分指引（9 marks）
  • Content 內容 (4)：有寫週末活動、購物／學校設施／失物等相關內容；句子達 5–6 句。
  • Language 語文 (3)：動詞時態大致正確（現在式為主）；常用詞彙拼寫大致正確；冠詞／介詞偶有錯可扣 0.5–1。
  • Organisation 組織 (2)：意思清楚、句子之間有連貫；大小寫與句號基本正確。

Sample answer 參考範文：
On Saturday I go shopping with Mum. We buy bread at the bakery. On Sunday I play football in the park. My school has a big playground and a library. My classroom is next to the hall. Yesterday I lost my pencil. It is my pencil. I found it under the desk.

5. 施測建議

  • 先派學生卷，再播聽力；聽力約 8–10 分鐘。
  • 其餘部分約 40 分鐘；寫作可提醒學生先想再寫。
  • 若只考默書＋文法小測，可只取 Part B + Part C（45 分）作短測。
